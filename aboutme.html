About Marissa

Wielding my supply chain experience, I'm looking to break into the data analytics industry to help make data actionable, clear, and compelling! I'm a first-generation college graduate and I'd be honored to work with you!

Dig into my journey to this new career path below ⬇️

  • 🎢 Early Passion for Building & Organizing: Ever since I was a kid, I’ve been drawn to data-driven simulation games like Roller Coaster Tycoon and Zoo Tycoon. At just 10 years old, I was already analyzing patterns, optimizing map layouts, and tracking sales trends.
  • 🔭 Pattern Recognition & Insight Discovery: I have a natural curiosity for uncovering the “why” behind the data. I enjoy turning raw information into polished, meaningful insights and translating patterns into smarter strategies.
  • 📦 Supply Chain Experience: Years in logistics taught me the importance of detail, adaptability, and strategic thinking—skills I am eager to apply directly to data analytics.
  • 👩‍🏫 Joy of Teaching & Explaining: Helping others understand complex ideas is deeply rewarding and fuels my devotion for clear, accessible communication.
  • 🌱 Diverse Data Interests: Beyond work, I explore data in books, media, esports, tourism, ecosystems, and personal habits. These explorations help me see stories in the numbers.
  • 🥅 Driven by Purpose: I aim to support my family while enjoying life’s richness. Data analytics aligns perfectly with my professional goals and personal values.

Let’s Work Together! I’m excited to gain experience and help make sense of your data. Diving in and turning insights into impact is my passion! 🙂
